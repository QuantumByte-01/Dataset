# SECTION: 6. Control allocation
# START_PAGE: 17

The goal of control allocation is to map the virtual commands 
generated by the ﬂight controller to all the available actuators of 
the ﬂying vehicle. The redundancies between rotors, tilt angles 
and aerodynamic control surfaces need to be adequately employed, 
especially when the control authority depends on the ﬂight condi-
tions. As an example, the aerodynamic control surfaces have more 
control authority as the airﬂow speed passing over them increases.
